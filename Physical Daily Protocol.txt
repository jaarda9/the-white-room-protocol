* Monday (Strength Acquisition):

 Barbell Squat (5 sets: 10,8,6,6,4) - progressive overload
 Weighted Pull-ups (4x6-8) or Lat Pulldown
 Overhead Press (4x8-10)
 Face Pulls (3x15)
 Ab Wheel Rollouts (3x8)
 Cool-down (child's pose, deep breathing)

 Warm-up

To maintain the "Operator" physique within a 50-minute gym window, you must eliminate wasted time (scrolling, chatting, or wandering). This 3-day split focuses on **Force Production, Mechanical Advantage, and Structural Integrity.**

You will use **Supersets**—performing two exercises back-to-back with no rest—to maximize density.

---

### The 150-Minute Weekly Calibration

**General Protocol:**
* **Warm-up (5 mins):** Dynamic stretching or 500m row (high intensity).
* **Rest:** 60 seconds between Supersets. 
* **Intensity:** Every rep must be explosive on the way up, controlled on the way down.
* **Exit (5 mins):** Log your data and leave.

---

#### Day 1: Force Production (The Foundation)
*Focus: Vertical push/pull and lower body drive.*

| Order | Exercise | Sets/Reps | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A1** | **Barbell Back Squat** | 3 x 5-8 | Primary lower body power. |
| **A2** | **Pull-Ups (Weighted if possible)** | 3 x Max | Vertical pulling strength. |
| **B1** | **Overhead Press (Dumbbell or Bar)** | 3 x 8-10 | Shoulder stability and force. |
| **B2** | **Hanging Leg Raises** | 3 x 12 | Core compression/stability. |
| **C1** | **Farmer’s Walk (Heavy)** | 3 x 40m | Grip strength and posture. |

---

#### Day 2: Mechanical Advantage (The Pull)
*Focus: Posterior chain and horizontal power.*

| Order | Exercise | Sets/Reps | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A1** | **Deadlift (Conventional or Trap Bar)** | 3 x 5 | Raw kinetic output. |
| **A2** | **Dips (Chest Focus)** | 3 x 10-12 | Pushing power and triceps. |
| **B1** | **Bent Over Barbell Rows** | 3 x 8-10 | Back density and posture. |
| **B2** | **Dumbbell Lunges** | 3 x 10/leg | Single-leg stability/combat gait. |
| **C1** | **Face Pulls** | 3 x 15 | Rotator cuff and rear delt health. |
